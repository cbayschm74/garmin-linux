# Course transfer evidence

Last checked: 2026-10-15. This note separates Garmin's published instructions from tests on these two devices.

## Edge 1000

- Garmin's [GPX transfer guide](https://www.garmin.com/fr-FR/blog/comment-installer-une-trace-gpx-sur-votre-edge-et-tacx/) says to put a GPX track in `Garmin/NEWFILES` and explicitly lists the Edge 1000. The [Edge 1000 manual](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/edge1000/EN-US/GUID-3E9F932E-22BF-4454-8520-1EFCA047EF87.html) also documents sending a Connect course over USB and opening it under **Courses** after disconnecting.
- The connected Edge's internal volume has `Garmin/NewFiles` and six existing FIT courses in `Garmin/Courses`. The latter is an output/storage folder, not the tested import inbox.
- After a fresh verified backup on 2026-10-02, a synthetic five-point GPX track named `GLinux Test` was copied to internal `Garmin/NewFiles`. Source and destination SHA-256 matched; the volume was safely unmounted. The user disconnected and turned on the Edge, then confirmed `GLinux Test` appears under **Courses**. The test GPX and backup are private, outside Git.
- The guarded `import-edge-course` command was also tested on the physical Edge with a 556-point GPX derived from an existing backed-up FIT course. It made a fresh verified backup, copied and hash-checked the GPX, and safely unmounted. The user confirmed `GLinux Replay` appears under **Courses**. The exported route and backup remain private; this is a duplicate of an existing route for testing.

## fēnix 5X

- Garmin [documents importing GPX, FIT, or TCX courses into Connect](https://support.garmin.com/en-GB/?faq=wKuZXCaZRP4mWPX5aRz5h5), then using **Send to Device**. Its [course compatibility list](https://support.garmin.com/en-GB/?faq=ZY8vOwxq106cRyPSpiVbQ7) explicitly includes the fēnix 5X. The [fēnix 5X manual](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/fenix5x/EN-US/GUID-674C66B5-0784-4441-B456-E0313B5902D9.html) confirms a **Navigate → Courses** menu.
- The connected watch's `GarminDevice.xml` declares `GPSData` with GPX 1.1 input at `Garmin/NewFiles`, and FIT course (`FIT_TYPE_6`) input there with course output at `Garmin/Courses`. This is device-declared format and folder information; the Garmin manual does not describe this direct USB workflow.
- The backed-up `Garmin/GPX` files are waypoint collections, not course examples. After a fresh verified backup on 2026-10-02, a synthetic five-point GPX named `GLinux 5X Test` was copied to the declared inbox. Source and destination SHA-256 matched and the watch was safely unmounted. The user reported that **Courses** showed only **Create New** afterward. GPX input in the device XML therefore does not establish successful GPX-to-course conversion on this watch. The test GPX and backup are private, outside Git.
- On reconnection, the test GPX had disappeared from `Garmin/NewFiles`, while `Garmin/Courses` was empty. This shows the watch processed or discarded the file; it did not leave a visible course. After another fresh verified watch backup, a 627-byte FIT course produced by the Edge from our synthetic test route was copied to the watch's declared FIT course inbox. The copy hash matched, the watch was safely unmounted, and the user confirmed its internal name `GLinux Test` appeared under **Courses**. For this exact watch, tested direct USB transfer works with this FIT course; the tested GPX did not become a course.
- The guarded `import-fenix-course` command copied a second, distinct FIT course named `Ruta Posible 1` after its own fresh backup and hash check. The watch was safely unmounted, and the user confirmed the course appears in its **Courses** menu. This validates the guarded FIT import on the physical fēnix 5X.
- The project's GPX converter produced a 556-point FIT course named `GLinux Replay` from a private GPX of an existing Edge route. The guarded fēnix command made a fresh backup, copied and hash-checked the generated FIT, and safely unmounted. The user confirmed `GLinux Replay` appears under **Courses** on the watch. This verifies generated FIT acceptance for this route and watch; GPX timestamps, elevation, waypoints, and turn cues were not preserved by the converter.

## Transfer rule for the app

On 2026-10-15, a recorded October Edge ride was prepared privately as GPX and converted through the GUI to a 3,279-point FIT course named Edge Oct 11. Source positions and generated FIT were independently compared, then the reviewed GUI workflow made a fresh verified watch backup, copied and hash-verified the course, and safely unmounted. The user confirmed it appears, loads correctly, and renders on the fēnix map. Active navigation along the route has not been tested.

Before writing a course, detect the current model and volume, make a fresh complete backup, check the GPX/FIT preview, ensure the destination filename is absent, copy only into a documented or validated inbox, compare the copied hash, and safely unmount. Do not claim success until the course appears in the device menu.
